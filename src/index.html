Who I am

Engineer by trade, researcher by conviction.

Large Language Models Neuro-Symbolic AI MLOps NLP PyTorch vLLM Poincaré Embeddings Knowledge Graphs Google Cloud AWS Python RAG Systems Fine-Tuning LoRA / QLoRA

I'm a software engineer turned AI researcher, with over nine years of hands-on experience building systems that scale — from e-invoicing platforms processing millions of documents for Italian public administration, to production LLM pipelines handling 172,000+ insurance documents daily.

Today I work at Leithà (Unipol Group), where I lead the development of specialized extraction and classification pipelines powered by fine-tuned language models. My work bridges the gap between cutting-edge AI research and high-throughput production systems.

As a PhD candidate at Università Guglielmo Marconi, I investigate neuro-symbolic approaches to natural language understanding — specifically how hyperbolic geometry and symbolic knowledge structures can augment neural representations. My flagship project, JENeSyM, integrates WordNet with neural embeddings in a Poincaré manifold.

I've collaborated with Professor Guido Vetere on AI systems presented at IEEE MetroXRAINE and HHAI 2023, and I lecture on educational technologies at the postgraduate level. Research and engineering are not opposites for me — they are the same conversation.
